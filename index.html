BAND MANIFESTO

RISING FROM THE UNDERGROUND

Founded in 2018, StarX Live reshaped modern rock music by fusing heavy guitar riffage with high-octane stadium energy and futuristic synth landscapes. We are kicking off our global campaign by releasing 30 brand new original songs on YouTube, followed by live arena performances starting right from Hyderabad, India!

With 4 platinum-certified albums and over 12 million monthly listeners worldwide, StarX Live continues to push the boundaries of live rock performance.
